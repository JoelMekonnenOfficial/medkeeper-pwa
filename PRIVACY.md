# MedKeeper Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-06-02 _(initial release — update if you ship a materially different build)_

MedKeeper ("the app") is a personal medication-tracking application. This policy
explains exactly what data we collect, how it is stored, and your rights.
The short version is: we don't collect anything. Everything stays on your device.

---

## 1. What data we collect

**None.** MedKeeper does not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data
or health information.

We do not:

- ask for an account, email, phone number, or name;
- log analytics, usage statistics, or crash reports to any server;
- track which buttons you press or which screens you view;
- read your contacts, location, calendar, photos, or any other device data
  beyond the storage and notification APIs you explicitly grant.

The only information that exists is what **you** type into the app
(your medications, doses, schedules, and the timestamps when you tap
"Take" or "Skip"), and it lives in your browser's local IndexedDB store on
this device only.

## 2. Cookies and tracking

MedKeeper uses **no cookies, no tracking pixels, no fingerprinting, and no
third-party scripts**. The app contains zero external network calls at
runtime; all assets (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, icons, fonts) are served from
the same origin and cached by the service worker for offline use.

System fonts are used; we do not embed Google Fonts or any other remote
typography service.

## 3. Third-party services

**None.** MedKeeper does not integrate with any third-party SDK, ad network,
analytics platform, A/B testing service, attribution provider, push-notification
service, error reporter, or social login.

Notifications, if you enable them, are generated by your operating system's
local Notification API. No notification content is sent through a third-party
push server (e.g. Apple's APNs or Google's FCM are not used for content
delivery; notifications are scheduled locally on your device by the app's
service worker).

## 4. How data is stored

All medication data, dose logs, and settings live in **IndexedDB** in your
browser, scoped to the MedKeeper origin on this device. No cloud sync, no
remote backup, no server copy.

When you ask the app to **Export backup**, it produces a JSON file that you
can save where you choose (using the File System Access API on supported
browsers, or a standard download otherwise). When you ask the app to
**Share schedule**, it hands a plain-text string to your operating system's
share sheet so you can send it via the channel of your choice (Messages,
email, etc.). The app does not call any network endpoint during either action.

## 5. Data retention

Your data is retained on this device **indefinitely**, until you delete it.

You can delete:

- a single medication (which also deletes its dose logs), via **Meds > Edit > Delete**;
- a single dose log, via **Today > Undo**;
- **everything** at once, via **Settings > Data > Clear all data**
  (requires a double confirmation).

If you uninstall the app or clear your browser's site data, all MedKeeper
data on this device is removed.

## 6. Security

- All storage is local to your browser, sandboxed to the MedKeeper origin.
- An **optional 4-digit PIN** can be enabled in Settings. It is salted and
  hashed locally with SHA-256 via the Web Crypto API; the salt is generated
  with `crypto.getRandomValues`. The plaintext PIN is never stored.
- There is intentionally **no PIN recovery**. If you forget your PIN, you
  must clear local data and start over. This is a deliberate privacy
  trade-off: there is no server-side reset because there is no server.
- Use your device's OS-level lock (Face ID, fingerprint, passcode) as an
  additional layer of protection.

## 7. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Because MedKeeper does not collect or process your data on any server,
the legal questions of "controller", "processor", and cross-border transfer
do not arise. You always control your data.

You have the right to:

- **Access** your data: open the app — that's it.
- **Export** your data: Settings > Data > Export backup produces a complete
  JSON file in a portable, human-readable format.
- **Delete** your data: per-medication, per-log, or "Clear all data".
- **Object** to any processing: there is none to object to.

These rights are exercised directly inside the app; no email request is
required because no one else holds your data.

## 8. Children's policy

MedKeeper is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly
collect data from anyone of any age (see Section 1). If a parent or
guardian uses MedKeeper to track a minor's medications, all data remains
on the parent's device and is subject to the same protections. We comply
with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) by virtue of
collecting nothing.

## 9. Changes to this policy

If we change anything about how the app handles data, we will update this
document and bump the **Effective date** above. Because there is no
remote endpoint and no user account, we cannot email you about changes;
please re-read this policy after major version updates if you want to
confirm nothing has changed. We will state, in the **What's New** notes
for any release that affects privacy, the nature of the change.
